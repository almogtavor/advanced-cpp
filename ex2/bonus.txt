Bonus features requested for Assignment 2
==========================================

1) Support for different output-resolution requests
---------------------------------------------------
The simulator honours the mission's optional `output_mapping_resolution_factor`
and reports the outcome per the assignment's three states:

  - factor missing / integer >= 1 : ACCEPTED, output resolution = gps_resolution
                                     * factor (a coarser output map is produced
                                     and saved at that resolution).
  - factor < 1                     : IGNORED_TOO_SMALL, falls back to
                                     gps_resolution.
  - non-integer factor >= 1        : IGNORED, falls back to gps_resolution.

The decided resolution and the request status are both written into
simulation_output.yaml (resolution_cm and resolution_request_status), and the
output .npy is genuinely produced at the accepted resolution.

How to check:
  ./drone_mapper_simulation_test --gtest_filter=SimulationRun.Resolution*
  ./drone_mapper_simulation_test --gtest_filter=Integration.ResolutionFactorReflectedInReport

The integration test runs a full simulation with output_mapping_resolution_factor
= 2 and asserts the report shows ACCEPTED and an output resolution of 20cm
(gps_resolution 10cm x 2), demonstrating a real coarser output map end to end.
